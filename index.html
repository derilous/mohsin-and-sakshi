The order of events

Celebrations

Day One

Haldi

Friday, 19 March 2027

3:30 pm — 6:30 pm

The turmeric ceremony. Family and friends bless the bride and groom with haldi paste for good fortune and a bright start. Wear something you don’t mind staining — yellow is traditional, and inevitable.

Day Two

Nikah

Saturday, 20 March 2027

4:00 pm — 6:00 pm

The marriage ceremony, in which the vows are exchanged and the marriage contract is signed before our families. Please arrive a little early so we can begin together.

Day Two

Reception

Saturday, 20 March 2027

7:00 pm — 10:00 pm

Dinner, speeches and dancing under the Bali sky. The evening runs straight on from the Nikah, so stay exactly where you are.

Before you travel

Your Stay

Accommodation

We are holding a block of rooms at The Westin for our guests. Booking details and rates will be shared here closer to the date — we will email everyone who has RSVP’d.

Visas

Most visitors may enter Indonesia on a Visa on Arrival, valid for 30 days. Requirements change, so please check the rules for your own passport well before booking.

When To Arrive

We suggest arriving by Thursday 18 March so you are rested for the Haldi, and leaving on Sunday 21 March or later. Bali is worth the extra days.

Weather

March in Bali is warm and humid — around 30°C by day, with the occasional short downpour. Pack light fabrics, and something for the evening breeze by the water.

What to wear

Dress Code

Tropical formal, with a leaning toward Indian occasion wear. Think breathable fabrics and soft colour — it will be warm, and there will be dancing. Flat or block heels are your friend on sand and stone.
