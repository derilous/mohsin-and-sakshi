Before you travel

Your Stay

We have booked and are covering accommodation for our guests — there is nothing for you to arrange or pay for.

Where You’ll Stay

Mohsin’s family and guests will be at The Westin, where the celebrations are held. Sakshi’s family and guests will be in a private villa close by. We will send you your own booking details once you have RSVP’d.

If you would rather arrange your own stay, you are very welcome to — just tell us in your RSVP so we don’t hold a room for you.

When To Arrive

Please plan to arrive on the morning of Friday 19 March. That is when your accommodation begins, and it leaves you time to settle before the Haldi at half past three.

We suggest flying home on Sunday 21 March or later. Bali is worth the extra days.

Visas

Most visitors may enter Indonesia on a Visa on Arrival, valid for 30 days. Requirements change, so please check the rules for your own passport well before booking.

Weather

March in Bali is warm and humid — around 30°C by day, with the occasional short downpour. Pack light fabrics, and something for the evening breeze by the water.

What to wear

Dress Code

Friday · Haldi

Wear Yellow

Yellow, head to toe. Marigold, mustard, butter, turmeric — any shade you like, as long as it is yellow. Choose something you don’t mind losing to the haldi.

Saturday · Wedding Day

Tropical Formal

Indian occasion wear, or formal in light, breathable fabrics. It will be warm and there will be dancing, so flat or block heels will serve you far better than stilettos on sand and stone.

Please, no white.
